# Research Radar

AI research is really fun, but there's no denying that it's becoming increasingly difficult to keep up with what's being produced. Everyday is a struggle to make sure you're still relevant and you research wasn't just outperformed by some new model. This goes beyond AI research, where the idea is there's massive information overload and you need to know what's good quality and truly worth a deeper dive.

Here's my way of solving this: a workflow to watch your research subfield on a schedule. Every 5 days new publications (arXiv, the works) get filtered by semantic similarity to your research question. Then an LLM scores each paper on relevance, reusable methodology, citation value, and what the paper results imply for your work's feasibility/credibility; held in an SQLite db and presented as a markdown digest.
